DATE AND TIME FUNCTIONS:-
------------------------
CURDATE()--> CURRENT DATE.
CURTIME()--> CURRENT TIME.
NOW(); CURRENT DATE AND TIME.
DATE();- EXTRACTED DATE PART.
TIME();- EXTRACTED TIME PART.
YEAR();- EXTRACT YEAR.
MONTH();-EXTRACT MONTYH.
DAY()/DAY OF MONTH.
WEEK():- EXTRACT WEEK NUMBER.
DAYOFWEEK(): EXTRACT DAY OF THE WEEK.
DATE_ADD(): ADD TIME TO A DATE.
DATE_SUB(): SUBTRACT TIME FROM A DATE.
DATEDIFF(): DIFFRENCE BETWEEN TWO DATES.
TIMESTAMPDIFF(): DIFFRENCE BETWEEN TWO DATETIME VALUES.
